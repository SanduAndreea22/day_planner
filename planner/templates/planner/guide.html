🌸 How Emotional Planner works

A short tour — no pressure to use everything at once.

🌤 Today

Your daily page. Add a few time blocks for the day, check them off as you go, and jot down notes whenever you feel like it. There’s no "right" way to fill it in — a single line is enough.

🌙 Evening reflection

Close the day gently: pick a mood and a color, and optionally note what drained you and one small win. Once a day is closed, it’s locked in as-is — a quiet snapshot of how it went. You can turn on an evening reminder email for this in your profile.

📅 Calendar

See every day at a glance, colored by mood. Click any day to open it and add or review details.

📈 Week & 🧭 Month

The weekly score is a gentle summary of how balanced the week felt — not a grade. The monthly overview shows your dominant mood and a short reflection on the month as a whole.

🔍 Search

Look back through past notes by keyword, or filter by mood, to find a day you’re thinking of.

📦 Export your data

From your profile, you can download everything you’ve written as a zip of CSV files, any time.

💬 Feedback

This app is still evolving. If something felt confusing, broken, or missing, tell us on the feedback page — we read every message.
