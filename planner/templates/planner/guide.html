How Emotional Planner works

Use as much or as little as you like. One short entry is enough.

  1. 1Check inTap how you feel.
  2. 2Write an entryA few lines are enough.
  3. 3Close the dayLock it in when you're done.

Today

Your daily page: a mood, a journal entry, and the day's plan in time blocks.

More about today

Add a few time blocks (slots like 09:00 – 10:00 · Study), check them off as you go, and write a journal entry whenever you like. There’s no right way to fill it in: use as much or as little as you like.

Evening reflection

Close the day with two optional questions. Closing locks the day, so it can’t be edited afterward. Set your mood and color on Today first.

More about evening reflection

Write what drained you and one small thing you appreciate (both optional). Pick your mood and color on the Today page first, because they can’t be changed once the day is closed.

Calendar

Every day at a glance: the mood as an emoji, tinted with the day’s color.

More about calendar

Tap any day to open it and add or review details.

Week & month

See which days you checked in and how the month’s moods add up. No ranking.

More about the week and month

The week page summarizes the days you logged and links to your mood chart and a chart of finished time blocks. The month summary (reachable from the Calendar page) shows your most common mood and a short note on the month as a whole.

Search

Find any day by what you wrote or how you felt.

More about search

Search your journal entries by keyword, or filter by mood.

Export your data

Download all your entries as spreadsheet files (CSV), any time.

More about exporting

From your profile, you get a file you can open in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app.
