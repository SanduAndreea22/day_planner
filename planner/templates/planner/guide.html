A short tour

How Emotional Planner works

No pressure to use everything at once. A single line is enough.

  1. 1Check inTap how you feel.
  2. 2Write a lineOne thought is enough.
  3. 3Close the dayWhen you're ready, gently.

Today

Your daily page: a mood, a thought and the day's plan in time blocks.

More

Add a few time blocks for the day, check them off as you go, and jot down notes whenever you feel like it. There’s no "right" way to fill it in — a single line is enough.

Evening reflection

Close the day with two optional questions. Once closed, it stays as it was.

More

Write what drained you and one small thing you appreciate (both optional). Pick your mood and color on the Today page first, because they can’t be changed once the day is closed.

Calendar

Every day at a glance, colored by mood.

More

Click any day to open it and add or review details.

Week & month

A calm summary of what you recorded. No ranking.

More

The week page gives a gentle summary of the days you logged and links to your mood and completed-tasks charts. The month summary (reachable from the Calendar page) shows your dominant mood and a short reflection on the month as a whole.

Search

Find a day you're thinking of.

More

Look back through past notes by keyword, or filter by mood.

Export your data

Download everything you've written, any time.

More

From your profile, you get a file you can open in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app.

This app is still evolving. If something felt confusing, broken, or missing, tell us on the feedback page. We read every message.
