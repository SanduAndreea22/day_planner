🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Emotional Planner ("we", "us", "the app") is operated by Andreea Sandu, an individual based in Romania, not a registered company. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible for your data

Andreea Sandu is the data controller for Emotional Planner. For any question or request about your data, contact lexu22deea@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile data (optional): a nickname and a short bio, if you choose to add them.
  • Journal data: the days you log, your mood, an "energy" color, free-text notes, daily schedule entries, and evening reflections (what drained your energy, a small win) — whatever you choose to write.
  • Technical data: standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, request timestamps) kept temporarily by our hosting provider for security and reliability, not used for tracking or profiling.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone, ever.

3. Why we process your data

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (storing and showing you your own journal entries), to authenticate you, and to send you account-related emails such as password resets. The legal basis is performance of the contract formed when you create an account, and your consent for the optional profile fields.

4. Who else sees your data

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They only process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Render — hosts the application itself.
  • Neon — hosts our Postgres database, where your journal data is stored.
  • Brevo — sends transactional emails (such as password reset links) on our behalf.
  • Google Fonts — loading the app's font may cause your browser to request it directly from Google's servers, which can see your IP address at that moment. No account data is shared with Google.

5. Cookies and local storage

We only use strictly necessary cookies: a session cookie to keep you logged in, and a CSRF cookie to protect your account against cross-site request forgery. We also store your light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. None of these are used for tracking or advertising, so no cookie-consent banner is required.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and everything in it at any time from your profile page — this is immediate and cannot be undone.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (via your profile page).
  • Erase your data (self-service account deletion, or by contacting us).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Romania, the ANSPDCP.

8. Age requirement

Emotional Planner is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Not medical advice

Emotional Planner is a personal journaling tool, not a medical or mental health service. If you're in crisis or need professional support, please contact a qualified professional or emergency services in your country.
